# Dashboard assets

## card-alert.gif

Slack's `:alert:` emoji: the red rotating siren light (128 × 128 GIF, 17 frames).
The dashboard draws it 18px square in the head of a task card past one core, just before the node pill
(`@2lab.ai/ui` 0.51.0 `data.alertIcon`, served at `/dashboard/assets/card-alert.gif`).

- Source: slackmojis.com, emoji "alert"
  (https://emojis.slackmojis.com/emojis/images/1643514276/2453/alert.gif), a free emoji collection for Slack
  workspaces. It is not original to this repository and carries no license statement of its own.
- If you need an image with a known license, replace this file; without it the card shows 🚨 (no image is drawn).
